Privacy Policy for Just Drafts

Effective September 24, 2026

Just Drafts is a local-first Android writing app published by eldal. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Draft content

Drafts are stored on your Android device. Just Drafts does not operate an advertising or analytics service and does not send your draft content to the developer.

If you choose to connect Google Drive or Dropbox, the app sends backup files to the private app-storage area of the service you selected. Cloud backup is optional. Your use of those services is also governed by the privacy policy and terms of the selected provider.

Account connections

When you connect Google Drive or Dropbox, the app receives authorization credentials needed to access its own private app-storage area. Those credentials are stored on your device and are used only to provide backup and restore functions. Just Drafts does not request access to unrelated files in your cloud account.

Encryption and device protection

The paid version offers optional AES-256-GCM encryption for local drafts and cloud backup files, plus optional biometric or device-lock access. Encryption keys are created and protected by the Android Keystore. An encrypted cloud backup currently depends on the key held by the device that created it, so it may not be recoverable after the key is lost or from another device.

Internet connections to Google Drive, Dropbox, and Google Play use the secure transport provided by those services.

Purchases and trial

The app provides a seven-day local trial. Trial status is stored on the device. Lifetime purchases are processed by Google Play. The developer does not receive or store your full payment-card details.

Data retention and deletion

Local drafts and settings remain on the device until you delete them, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app. You can delete drafts inside the app. Cloud backup data remains in the connected provider's private app storage until it is replaced, removed through the provider, or the app's access or data is revoked according to that provider's controls.

Children

Just Drafts is a general-purpose productivity app and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

This policy may be updated as features or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are made.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the support email shown on the Just Drafts Google Play listing.
